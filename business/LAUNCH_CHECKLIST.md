# Launch checklist

## Before taking payments
- [ ] Verify business name, domain, and any trademark conflicts.
- [ ] Select an appropriate business structure and register if necessary.
- [ ] Obtain EIN where appropriate and open business banking.
- [ ] Verify Florida/local business tax, licensing, and any sales tax obligations.
- [ ] Confirm business insurance with a qualified broker.
- [ ] Establish written client contracts and invoice/payment procedures.

## Before exposing the website publicly
- [ ] Replace BUSINESS_EMAIL with a monitored address.
- [ ] Generate a unique 32+ character ADMIN_PASSWORD.
- [ ] Place the website behind HTTPS using a business domain.
- [ ] Restrict `/admin` behind VPN/identity proxy where feasible.
- [ ] Enable reverse-proxy rate limiting, abuse filtering, request-size limits, and security headers.
- [ ] Add a customized privacy notice and inquiry retention/deletion rules.
- [ ] Confirm offsite backups and test restore.
- [ ] Test the inquiry process end to end (and regularly check the dashboard).
- [ ] Assess accessibility, client contracts, and any legal/regulatory obligations.

## First customer
- [ ] Define a narrowly scoped offer.
- [ ] Interview 10 potential customers.
- [ ] Prepare pricing based on estimated time, overhead, and risk.
- [ ] Send a written proposal and execute a contract.
- [ ] Deliver, test, document, get sign-off, and ask permission for a testimonial.
